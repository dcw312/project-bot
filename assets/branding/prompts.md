# Bot Branding — Image Generation Prompts

Place generated files here:
- `icon.png` — 1024×1024
- `banner.png` — 680×240

---

## Icon (1024×1024)

> Cute English Cream Golden Retriever puppy sitting upright, holding a small clipboard or checklist in its paws, soft warm cream fur, friendly expression, minimal flat-style illustration, pastel warm background, centered composition, no text, digital art, Discord bot icon style

**Tips:**
- Request square output (1:1)
- Ask for "flat illustration" or "vector style" for a clean icon look
- If the model supports style references, aim for something like a Notion or Linear mascot aesthetic

---

## Banner (680×240)

> Wide panoramic illustration of an English Cream Golden Retriever lounging at a desk with a laptop, sticky notes, and a to-do list, warm cream and golden tones, soft gradient background in muted blue or warm beige, relaxed and friendly vibe, minimal text space on the right side, no text, digital art, wide banner format

**Tips:**
- Request 17:6 aspect ratio or "wide banner" output — then crop/resize to 680×240
- Keep the dog on the left side so there's room for a bot name overlay on the right if needed
- "Soft gradient" or "studio lighting" keeps it clean for a Discord banner
